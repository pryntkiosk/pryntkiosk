# 💫 About Me:
I am Avinash Rajendra Verma, an aspiring entrepreneur and engineering student, and the founder of Prynt. I am passionate about building technology-driven solutions that solve real-world problems. I have experience in full-stack web development, product design, and startup planning, with skills in React, Node.js, Express, and MongoDB. Through Prynt, I aim to make document printing more affordable and accessible by developing smart self-service printing kiosks integrated with digital payments and cloud technology.
